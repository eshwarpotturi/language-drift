An audit of five AI assistants in three languages

Would your company's AI give the same advice in every language?

We asked five popular AI assistants the same questions in English, Hindi and Chinese. Same AI, same question, only the language changed. Sometimes the advice flipped.

5What this means for a business

Test your AI in every language your people use.An assistant that behaves well in English may give different advice in Hindi or Chinese. Check it the way you'd check a new policy document in translation.
Keep a person on the big decisions.The biggest differences showed up on hiring, firing, lending and pricing. Use AI to draft and explain, and let a person decide.
Re-check whenever you switch AI.Some AIs were steady across languages and others were not. The choice of AI matters, so test before you roll out a new one.
